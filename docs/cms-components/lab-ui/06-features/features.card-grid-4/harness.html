Unified Platform

What ServiceWand Replaces

Most service businesses run their operations across 4–6 separate tools that don't speak to each other. ServiceWand replaces the stack with a single connected system.

Each replaced surface keeps its strengths and adds the context the others lacked.

The result is fewer tabs, fewer copies of the same data, and less context-switching during the day.

01

Scheduling Tool

ServiceWand handles scheduling, dispatch, routes, and recurring service in one calendar.

Constraint-aware so the calendar respects skills, vehicles, and territories automatically.

02

Field Mobile App

A full field operations app — not a read-only schedule. Techs complete work, capture, invoice, and update from anywhere.

Offline-capable so connectivity gaps don't stop the day.

03

CRM & Billing

Properties, contacts, contracts, invoices, and payments live with the operational data — no separate CRM or billing console.

Recurring contracts and multi-currency billing handled natively.

04

Reporting & Automation

Live dashboards, drill-through to underlying work, and rule-based automations.

AI assists where the data is rich enough to be useful — not just for show.
