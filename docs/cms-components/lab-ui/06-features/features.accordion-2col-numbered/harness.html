How It Works

Field Service Software That Powers Real Field Operations

ServiceWand connects every part of a field service workflow. Scheduling, dispatching, mobile workforce coordination, customer management, billing, reporting, and AI-assisted operations all run from one operational core.

Service businesses use ServiceWand to keep their daily work moving even when conditions change.

Built for crews, routes, recurring service, emergency dispatch, multi-location teams, and customer experiences that span office, field, and digital.

Plan crew capacity, recurring routes, and emergency dispatch from one calendar. Constraints — skills, vehicles, territories, time windows — are respected automatically.
Techs run the day from a phone — jobs, notes, photos, signatures, parts, invoicing. Works offline and syncs when connection returns.
Generate invoices from completed work, recurring contracts, or material usage. Tax, multi-currency, and partial-payment workflows handled in one place.
Trigger reminders, follow-ups, renewals, and escalation flows based on status, time, or business rules — without hand-built integrations.
Define service territories, optimize multi-stop routes, and rebalance loads when teams or vehicles change.
Track stock across warehouses, trucks, and job sites. Trigger reorders when usage drops thresholds.
Customers see upcoming visits, invoices, service history, and contracts — and self-serve approvals and payments.
Granular role and territory permissions, SSO, audit log. Each user sees only the work and data they're authorized for.
Route changes, reassignments, and ETA updates push to the field instantly. Dispatchers see who is where and what's next.
Every property, contact, contract, recurring service, and history is stored against the customer so the next visit picks up exactly where the last one ended.
Dashboards for revenue, crew productivity, route efficiency, customer churn, and operational health. Drill into any number to the underlying work order.
Schedule suggestions, anomaly alerts, and natural-language search across jobs, customers, and history.
Model seasonal routes, annual service plans, and tiered contracts. ServiceWand generates the work, the bills, and the renewals on schedule.
Send branded estimates from the field or the office. Approved quotes turn into jobs without re-entry.
Accounting, payments, mapping, communications — connect the systems you already use rather than replacing them.
