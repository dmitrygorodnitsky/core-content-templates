section.stages-list · live
Stage 01

Solo & Owner-Operator

1–3 users

You and a couple of techs. One calendar, a customer list, invoicing on Fridays. The foundation that has to keep working as everything else grows around it.

Scheduling Customer history Estimates Mobile capture Invoicing
Stage 02

Small Service Business

4–15 users

Multiple crews on the road. Recurring work, route context, customer communication, accounting integration. The point where most teams outgrow their first tool and start the migration cycle.

+Dispatch board +Recurring service +Customer portal +Route optimization
Stage 03

Growing Operation

16–60 users

Service territories, multiple offices, dispatchers handling escalations all day. The team needs operational visibility, automation for the repetitive, and reporting beyond completed-jobs counts.

+Territories +Automation rules +Live dashboards +Asset history
Stage 04

Multi-Location Enterprise

60+ users

Multiple regions, complex contracts, integrations everywhere, governance and audit requirements. The platform has to disappear into the background of how the business operates.

+Multi-region +Role-based access +Audit log +AI agents
