AI-Native Operations

Field Service Software That Thinks With You

ServiceWand uses AI where the operational data is rich enough for it to be genuinely useful — schedule suggestions, anomaly alerts, natural-language search across jobs, properties, and history.

Not a chatbot wrapper, not generic LLM marketing. AI features are surfaced inside the actual workflows your team already runs.

You stay in control of what the system suggests and what gets accepted. AI augments operational judgment; it does not replace it.
