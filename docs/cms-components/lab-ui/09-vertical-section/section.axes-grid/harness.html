section.axes-grid · live
Axis 01Locations
Single office→Multi-branch · multi-region

Service businesses scale from a single office to multiple branches and eventually multi-region operations. ServiceWand supports each shape — one team, several territories, or many regions with separate data residency.

Axis 02Crews
1 owner + apprentice→Hundreds of techs across crews

Crews grow from a couple of techs into territory-level workforce. Scheduling, dispatch, mobile workflows, and reporting all need to keep working at every count without a tool swap.

Axis 03Customer Complexity
One-off residential calls→Recurring contracts · multi-property

Customer models evolve from individual one-off jobs to recurring contracts, multi-property accounts, and tiered service agreements. The customer model supports all of those without redesigning the data.

Axis 04Billing Surface
Invoice on completion→Subscriptions · partial payments · multi-currency

Billing starts simple (invoice when the job is done) and gets richer as operations grow: recurring contracts, partial payments, multi-currency, accounting integrations, and audit trails.

Axis 05Operational Visibility
End-of-week summary→Live dashboards · drill-through

Reporting starts as a Friday spreadsheet and ends as live operational dashboards across routes, crews, billing, customer satisfaction, and asset performance — drillable to the underlying work order.

Axis 06Integrations
Standalone→ERP · CRM · payments · maps · comms

Most operations start standalone and gradually connect into the rest of the business: accounting, CRM, mapping, payments, communications. ServiceWand connects through APIs, webhooks, and configurable workflows.
