# Field Service Software for Real-World Operations

ServiceWand connects scheduling, dispatching, routing, mobile work, billing, reporting, and customer management in one operational platform.

It is built for service businesses that coordinate crews, customers, routes, recurring work, emergency requests, and operational decisions throughout the day.

## Features

ServiceWand gives teams one operating layer for office, field, and customer workflows.

### Scheduling and Dispatch

Plan appointments, crew capacity, recurring routes, and emergency work from one live operational calendar.

### Mobile Workforce

Give field teams job context, customer history, notes, photos, signatures, and invoices from a mobile workflow.

### Customer Management

Keep properties, contacts, service history, contracts, and customer communication connected to the work being performed.

### Billing and Reporting

Turn completed work into invoices and operational reporting without re-entering data across systems.

## FAQ

### Can ServiceWand support multiple service lines?

Yes. ServiceWand is designed for teams that share scheduling, dispatching, routing, billing, and reporting foundations across multiple service models.

### Does ServiceWand replace a CRM?

It can own field-service operational CRM workflows or integrate with an existing CRM, depending on how your organization is structured.

### Can crews use ServiceWand from the field?

Yes. Mobile workflows keep technicians connected to job details, customer history, notes, photos, signatures, and billing steps.

### Is this only for one vertical?

No. The same operating model can support snow removal, HVAC, lawn care, cleaning, plumbing, electrical, pest control, and other field service operations.
