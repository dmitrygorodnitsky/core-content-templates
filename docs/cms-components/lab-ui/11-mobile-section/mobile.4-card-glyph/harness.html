Field Mobile App

Built for the Field, Not for the Desk

The ServiceWand field app is a full operations surface, not a read-only schedule view.

Designed for the realities of working outside — gloves, connectivity gaps, noisy environments, time pressure.

Techs complete real work without bouncing back to the office for status updates or re-entry.

Full Work-Order Capture

Notes, photos, parts, signatures, and time captured against the job.

No paper, no end-of-day rekeying back at the office.

Offline-Capable

Techs keep working with no connectivity — basements, rural sites, dead-zone neighborhoods.

Changes sync automatically once the device comes back online.

Live Route Updates

Reassignments, new urgent stops, ETAs, and customer notes push to the phone immediately.

Dispatchers don't have to call to confirm the tech saw the change.

On-Site Invoicing

Generate, present, and take payment for invoices from the phone while the work is still in the customer's mind.

Same data flows back to billing and accounting without re-entry.
