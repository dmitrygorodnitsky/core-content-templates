## C2 — Sellability from inventory

Commit: `feat(shop): take sellability from Core inventory and nothing else`, on
`codex/lab-ui-durable-catalog`.

### What landed

`core-pim /api/inventory/list.json` is now joined to the catalog inside
`runtime/src/adapters/core-pim-adapter.js`, and every product carries one
normalized field:

| `sellability` | meaning | `cart.addItem` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sellable` | a `SPA_STOCK` row reports a positive count | present |
| `out-of-stock` | a `SPA_STOCK` row reports zero | removed |
| `unknown` | no row exists, or inventory could not be read | removed |

**The cart API does not enforce stock.** Verified live on staging 2026-07-28 by
the operator: adding the zero-count `CHS_SKIN_002` to the cart returns `200` and
Core creates the line. There is no server-side refusal behind this join — it is
the only thing standing between a customer and a purchase the studio cannot
fulfil, which is why the buy action is closed in the envelope rather than left
to a downstream guard. Both the adapter header and `pim-adapter-check.mjs` say
this in as many words, so a later reader does not assume Core would catch it.

The join key is the inventory row's `product` relation **id**, requested in the
`{key:"id", mappings, name, type:"identifier"}` form. `Inventory` has no `code`
column; the row's `RECORD_CODE` attribute is seed identity and is never treated
as a product reference, and `notes` is never identity at all.

Narrowing to `SPA_STOCK` is a server filter on `type.code` — a real relation
column, the same shape the shipment and enrolment reads already use. It is
deliberately not a filter on `attributes.*`: Core cannot filter dynamic
attributes and fails silently, returning `200` with zero rows, which would read
as "nothing is in stock" rather than as an error. A client-side guard drops any
returned row whose type is present and is not `SPA_STOCK`.

`pimEnrichment` gained an `inventory` member alongside `models` and `reviews`
(`ready | unavailable | error | session-expired | forbidden`), so a shelf-wide
inventory failure can be told apart from a catalog with no rows.

### Decisions taken under ambiguity

1. **The stock count is not exposed.** The objective asked for one normalized
   sellability field; exposing the raw count would be the first ingredient of a
   low-stock badge the accepted design does not define. Presentation gets the
   verdict, not the number.

2. **`unknown` closes the buy action and renders nothing.** The accepted design
   has treatments for `out-of-stock` (an "Out of stock" chip on a muted card)
   and for `unavailable` (a chip reading "Not sold online"). Neither is true of
   a product whose stock nobody stated, so no markup was improvised. Brief filed:
   `app-templates/customer-portal/design-requests/calm-harbor-unknown-stock-shop-state.md`.

3. **The inventory join is gated on `pimEnrichmentMode === "current-api"`**, the
   flag that already gates the authenticated PIM reads, rather than on a new
   config key. Closed enrichment makes no inventory call and leaves every
   product `unknown` — a deployment with no inventory contract cannot honestly
   claim stock. No `config.js` change was needed, so `runtime/manifest.json`
   `fileInventory` is untouched.

4. **A failed inventory read makes everything `unknown`, never `sellable`.**
   Availability is never assumed from the absence of a refusal.

5. **`core-pim-enrichment-check.mjs` was extended, not forked** — three lines:
   an empty inventory response in its stub, the `inventory` member in its two
   enrichment-state assertions, and one assertion that an empty inventory read
   leaves stock `unknown`. It is the second check over the same adapter, and the
   zone rule for `scripts/*-check.mjs` is "extend, do not fork".

`pim-adapter-check.mjs` proves, in one run: an in-stock product is `sellable`
and keeps `cart.addItem`; a zero-count product is `out-of-stock` and loses it; a
product with no row is `unknown`, is not `out-of-stock`, and loses it; all three

inventory call.

### Residuals

1. **The Shop still renders `sellable` for everything in live mode.**
   `state.js:156-165` `spaSellInfo()` hardcodes `{ state: "sellable" }` for any
   product found in the live envelope. `state.js` is the C4 hotspot and was out
   of bounds for this slice, so the adapter now tells the truth and the selector
   still does not read it. **Handoff to C4:** `spaSellInfo` should take
   `product.sellability` instead of the literal, which reaches the already-built
   `out-of-stock` treatment in `SpaShopPage.js:43` and
   `SpaProductDetailPage.js:136` with no new markup. Until then the zero-stock
   case is proven at the adapter and check level only.

   One detail worth knowing before wiring it: `SpaShopPage.js:28` mutes a card
   only for `out-of-stock` and `unavailable`, and `:42-55` emits a chip only for
   `out-of-stock`, `unavailable` and `variant-required`. Passing `unknown`
   straight through therefore renders an unmuted card with a **disabled**
   "Add to bag" and no explanation of why. That is a closed buy action, so it is
   acceptable as an interim, but it is the weakest of the three cases and is
   exactly what the design brief asks to replace. Do not improvise a chip for it
   in `SpaShopPage.js`.

2. **Unknown is the dominant live case, not an edge.** Inventory holds exactly
   two rows (`CHS_BODY_001` 12, `CHS_SKIN_002` 0) against 12 published
   `SPA_RETAIL` products. The other ten — `CHS_BODY_002/003/004/005`,
   `CHS_BATH_001/002`, `CHS_SKIN_001/003/004/005` — have no inventory row at
   all. Once residual 1 is wired, most of the shelf goes unbuyable: correct, but
   very visible. Two ways out, and they are not exclusive: the seed grows an
   inventory row per published retail product
   (`core-ui/scripts/dev/beautySpaEntities.ts`, one line per product, no new
   contract), and/or the design answers the `unknown` brief. This is a data gap,
   not a code gap; nothing was invented to paper over it.

3. **No live probe was run for this slice.** The join is proven against stubbed
   Core responses shaped from `evidence/S1.md` §5 (`Inventory` is
   `{product, count}`) and the recorded staging rows. A live read of
   `/api/inventory/list.json` as the customer needs the portal API key.

4. **`ARCHITECTURE.md:302`** describes `current-api` enrichment as ProductModel
   groups and published reviews; it does not yet mention the inventory join. The
   file is outside this slice's owned paths and was left alone.

## C3 — Cart presentation

Commit: `feat(cart): render the bag from the server cart envelope`, on
`codex/lab-ui-durable-catalog`.

**The `c3-wip` stash recorded in C2's section above was mine.** Owning it here
rather than leaving it anonymous: I ran `git stash push -m c3-wip --
app-templates/customer-portal/runtime app-templates/customer-portal/scripts` to
test whether a red `core-cart-adapter-check.mjs` predated my changes. The
pathspec looked narrow and was not — those two directories are the whole shared

The `git stash pop` then aborted because C1 had already rewritten
`core-cart-adapter.js` underneath it, leaving seven files reverted to HEAD.
Recovery was per-file `git show 'stash@{0}:<path>' > <path>` for those seven,
deliberately **excluding** C1's two files so their newer content survived. The

directory-wide revert of any kind, `git stash` included, and stage only exact
paths.

### What landed

A `cart` module (`runtime/src/modules/index.js`) selecting `createCoreCartAdapter()`
in live mode and an inert empty envelope otherwise, and a bag
(`runtime/src/routes/SpaCartPage.js`) that renders from that module's envelope
and from nothing else.

| bag state | live source | what renders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| unloaded | no `moduleData.cart` | the accepted skeleton — never fixture lines |
| `empty` | envelope with no lines | the accepted `cart-empty` block |
| `ready` | envelope with lines | item rows, a subtotal-only totals card, no fulfillment card |
| `error` | any failure but 403 | the accepted `error` gate |
| `unauthorized` | `cart-forbidden` (403) | the accepted `unauthorized` gate |

`session-expired` (401) additionally surfaces on the account, the way
`planModule` and `ordersModule` do. `cart-account-unknown` (404) maps to plain
`error`, not `unauthorized` — a bag that exists nowhere is not someone else's
bag, and saying "you don't have access" would be a lie.

`state.js` gained only the cart selector: `spaCartEnvelope()`, plus
`spaCartLines()` and `spaCartCount()` rewritten to read it. `spaCartCount()`
returns Core's `itemCount` verbatim in live mode — a quantity, not a line count
— and returns `0` rather than summing line quantities when Core states none.
Nothing else in that file was touched; `actions.js` was not opened.

### The money gap, and how it is gated

Core's `CartView` supplies **`subtotal` and nothing else**: no tax field, no
total field, no fulfillment dimension. The accepted totals card
(`design-inbox/src/routes/SpaCartPage.js:104-112`) has three rows and the
accepted fulfillment card (`:117-122`) has two bindings, all five of which were
being manufactured in the browser — `actions.js:318` `Math.round(subtotal * 0.08)`,
`:329` the assembled `displayTotals`, `:330` a hardcoded pickup constant. That is
audit finding `C-01`, routed to this wave.

In live mode the bag now renders Core's `displaySubtotal` verbatim, omits the Tax
and Total rows entirely, and omits the `cart-fulfillment` card entirely. No
figure is derived, summed, multiplied or rounded anywhere on the path. Both gaps
are briefed in
`app-templates/customer-portal/design-requests/calm-harbor-cart-server-subtotal-and-fulfillment.md`
and left gated until answered. **The producer at `actions.js:316-332` still
exists and still runs off live** — removing it is C4's, which owns that file.

### Decisions taken under ambiguity

1. **Two view decisions were extracted to `runtime/src/routes/spa-cart-view.js`**
   — `spaCartRouteState()` and `spaCartDisplayTotals()`. Both are pure and
   DOM-free, which is what lets `cart-module-check.mjs` assert "live totals are
   subtotal-only, and nothing computes the missing two" without a browser. A new
   file in `routes/` is a deviation from the 1:1 design transfer; it holds no
   markup and no copy, only the two decisions the page makes before it creates a
   node.

2. **`data-cart-ref` is omitted in live mode.** The accepted hook carries
   `state.spaCart.version`, a fixture construct. A live cart's only identifier is
   its raw Core id, and the commerce surfaces are explicitly non-enumerating —
   putting a raw id in the DOM to satisfy a hook would trade a real constraint
   for a test convenience. The fixture path keeps the hook unchanged. Recorded in
   the design brief's data-ownership section.

3. **A live deployment with the cart module disabled renders the accepted
   `unavailable` block** rather than an endless skeleton. This reuses the exact
   copy already configured in the page's own `spaGate` `unavailable` slot — no
   new treatment, no new copy. It is not a reachable configuration through the
   approved variants (`cart` is in `spaTarget.modules` and the export validates
   it), so this is a fail-honest guard, not a feature.

4. **A line whose money Core omitted keeps its row and shows nothing there.**
   `cart-row__total` and the unit-price line stay in the DOM as empty cells
   rather than being dropped, so the row's grid does not reflow. Rendering
   `"null each"` was the alternative the accepted code would have produced.

5. **The stale-price and inventory-conflict row states are fixture-only.** Core
   reports neither on a cart read, so `demo` is forced null in live mode and
   those branches — including the `ConflictBanner` and the blocked checkout
   button — are unreachable there. They are untouched off live.

### Residuals

1. **No live probe.** The wiring is proven against a stubbed `fetch` shaped from
   the live `CartView` the operator verified on 2026-07-28. Reading a real cart
   as the customer needs the portal API key.

2. **Line titles depend on the products module having loaded first.** The C1
   adapter joins a line's title from `state.moduleData.products`
   (`core-cart-adapter.js` `catalogTitle`), and `loadAllAsync` runs cart and
   products in parallel after `account`. If products lands second, the cart
   envelope keeps `productCode` as the title and does not re-resolve, because the
   envelope is normalized once at load. Cosmetic and self-correcting on the next
   cart read; a real fix is either an ordering rule in `portal-runtime.js` or a
   title resolved at render time, and neither is in this slice's zone.

3. **`spaCartLines()` now feeds the checkout page too.** `SpaCheckoutPage.js`
   imports it but does not currently call it — it reads `state.spaCart` directly
   at `:51`. In live mode that object is still the client-assembled one from
   `actions.js`, so checkout is unchanged by this slice and still shows assembled
   money. **Handoff to C4:** the selector is ready; the checkout quote should
   come from `spaCartEnvelope()` and the `displayTotals`/`fulfillment`
   fabrication at `actions.js:316-332` and `SpaCheckoutPage.js:45` should go with
   it.

4. **The bag badge reads `itemCount`, which is a quantity.** `SpaTopNav.js` and
   `SpaShopPage.js` render `spaCartCount()`. Off live it is still the sum of line
   quantities, which is the same number; nothing in either surface changed.

5. **`runtime/manifest.json` does not declare `cart-totals` or
   `cart-fulfillment`** as visual ids — a pre-existing gap the design audit
   recorded (`findings-components-commerce.md` §D1) and did not fix. Omitting the
   fulfillment card in live mode does not make it worse, but the manifest is
   still wrong about this surface.
