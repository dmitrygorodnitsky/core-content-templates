Who is ServiceWand for?

ServiceWand is built for snow and ice contractors coordinating multiple crews, vehicles, and commercial properties. It connects route planning with field work, customer service, quotations, and billing, with an entry plan for smaller teams.

Can I start with route optimization?

Yes. Advanced route optimization is available from Growth. You’ll set up the properties, resources, and service requirements the solver needs, and can introduce the connected field and office workflows as your team is ready.

Do I need to optimize routes for every snowfall?

No. You can reuse saved route templates for recurring service. When equipment availability or assignments change, you can reassign work and run optimization when it is needed.

What does the optimizer consider?

It can account for equipment capabilities, property requirements, and expected service time. You can control how long optimization runs and preserve selected stops while adjusting the rest of the plan.

Can I manage subcontractors alongside my own crews?

Yes, on Business and Enterprise. Coordinate assignments, GPS activity, work records, and related cases while limiting subcontractors to their own operational scope. They can manage their own people and resources within the permissions you define.

Can ServiceWand help measure properties and prepare quotations?

Business and Enterprise include area measurement and map-based quotations, with AI-assisted mapping capacity defined by the plan. Your team reviews the mapped areas and scope, then prepares the quotation using your services and pricing. Drawing service zones for field instructions is available on every plan.

Can I connect ServiceWand to my existing website?

Yes. Add a service request form or link to your website. Plans with Customer Portal also let you give customers a clear entry point to their documents, service information, and requests. Custom website work is scoped separately.

Can customers book snow service instantly?

The workflow begins with a service request. Your team reviews the property, requirements, and pricing before preparing a quotation and agreeing the service. The portal supports the customer relationship throughout that process.

Do we pay for every seasonal worker?

No. Every plan includes unlimited users. You can add, replace, or remove employees as your workforce changes. Subcontractor management is included on Business and Enterprise.

Can crews and customers use different languages?

ServiceWand supports localized interfaces and translated business information. AI-assisted translation can help with instructions, working information, customer requests, and replies. We’ll confirm the languages and translation capacity relevant to your operation during the demo.

How is completed work documented?

Crews can record work times, job status, GPS activity, before-and-after photos, and issues. These records stay connected to the property and visit so your team can review work and answer customer questions. Photo limits are defined per visit in the plan comparison.

Does ServiceWand support seasonal contracts and per-visit invoicing?

Yes. Invoicing can follow per-visit terms or seasonal billing schedules. Growth and higher plans add configured invoice generation and payment reminders. Customers can pay through Stripe.

Can you help us move from spreadsheets or another system?

Yes. We review the customer, property, workforce, vehicle, and equipment data you want to bring across, then agree preparation, migration, setup, and training scope. Complex cleanup or additional implementation work is quoted separately.

What if a client requires reports in another portal?

Share the requirements with us. We can assess a suitable report or integration, depending on the external system’s access and technical capabilities. Feasibility, deliverables, and fees are agreed before work begins.

Can we add more properties without changing plans?

Property expansion lets you add capacity within your current plan. A higher plan gives access to additional capabilities, such as optimization, subcontractor management, or map-based quotations. Capacity and expansion terms in this preview remain under review.

Understanding snow & ice operations

What is snow removal software?

Snow removal software helps contractors organize properties, resources, service visits, and customer records. ServiceWand connects those activities with route planning, field documentation, quotations, and invoicing in one operational platform.

What is the difference between route planning and route optimization?

Route planning lets a dispatcher build and assign a route. Optimization uses the properties, resources, and constraints provided to the system to calculate a route plan. Dispatchers still control the information, settings, and operational decisions around that plan.

Can the same system support snow clearing and de-icing?

Yes. Configure the services you provide, the resources they require, and the instructions for each property or service area. Keep the resulting work records connected to customer agreements and billing.
